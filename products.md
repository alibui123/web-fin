1. Products Page Structure (High-Level Overview)
The Products page should help visitors:
understand your product ecosystem
compare solutions quickly
identify the right product
navigate into detailed landing pages
This page should be concise and scannable.

Recommended Layout
A. Products Introduction
Short explanation of:
what your company builds
your AI-first approach
industries you target

B. Product Showcase Sections
Each product gets:
product name
short positioning statement
key capabilities
short AI highlight
“Explore Product” button
Not too much detail.
Think:
preview, not explanation.

C. Shared AI Capabilities
Explain the intelligence layer behind your products:
predictive analytics
automation
smart recommendations
AI assistants
workflow intelligence
reporting
This strengthens your company identity.

D. Industries Served
Show use cases and markets.

E. Final CTA
Encourage:
consultation
demos
product discovery
