2. Qualicall — AI Quality Assurance for Contact Centers
Most contact centers monitor only a fraction of calls due to the sheer volume of recordings and the limits of human reviewers. Qualicall changes that. It is an AI quality assurance agent that listens to every single call and evaluates it across the dimensions that matter most — script adherence, rebuttal handling, agent tone, customer sentiment, and call outcome.
The result is a complete, objective picture of how your team is performing at scale. Qualicall presents its findings through clean, role-specific dashboards: agents receive personal performance insights and concrete coaching suggestions, while management gets a higher-level view for workforce planning and training decisions. Every score and recommendation is fully explainable — Qualicall cites the exact moment in the call recording that informed its assessment, eliminating guesswork and building trust in the AI's findings. For businesses operating in regulated industries, Qualicall also provides a full, verifiable audit trail of every call's QA review — a compliance advantage that human spot-checking simply cannot replicate.
